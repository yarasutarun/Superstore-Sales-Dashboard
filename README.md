# Superstore-Sales-Dashboard
This repository contains an interactive Excel dashboard project that analyzes sales data from a fictional Superstore. The dashboard is designed to give insights into various performance metrics such as sales trends, customer orders, profit, delivery efficiency, and product category performance.  
### 🔹 **Overview Dashboard**  
- **Sales Trend Over Time** – Line chart showing monthly sales performance.  
- **Sales and Profit by Region** – Clustered bar chart comparing sales and profit region-wise.  
- **% of Sales by Category** – Donut chart to compare Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology.  
- **Sales by Sub-Category** – Tree map to identify top-performing product groups.  
- **Top 10 Cities by Sales** – Horizontal bar chart listing highest sales by city.  
- **% of Sales by Segment** – Donut chart breaking down sales by customer segment.  
- **% of Sales by Delivery Time** – Donut chart classifying delivery speed.  
- **KPIs** – Total Sales, Profit, Profit %, and Customer Count.  
### 🔹 **Daily Sales Dashboard**  
- **Day Wise Sales** – Column and line chart showing sales and number of orders by date.  
- **Sales by Day Type** – Donut chart showing breakdown by Weekday vs Weekend.  
- **Sales by Day of Week** – Bar chart comparing daily performance (Mon–Sun).  
- **KPIs** – Total Customers, Avg Sales, Avg Profit, Total Orders.  
- ## 🛠️ Features  
- Excel Power Query used for data transformation:  
  - Created columns for Year, Month, Day, Day Name.  
  - Delivery days and classification (Fast, Normal, Slow).  
  - Weekday/Weekend segmentation using conditional logic.  
- Pivot tables and slicers for interactivity.   
- Clean and professional UI with slicers for:  
  - Year, Month, Category, Segment, Region, Day.  
  - ## 📌 Tools Used  
- **Microsoft Excel**  
  - Power Query  
  - Pivot Tables  
  - Slicers  
  - Chart Customization  
  - ## 🚀 How to Use
